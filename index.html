Trip hacks before Japan ✈️

🧳 Luggage forwarding20 Oct: ask BON / Yamato about sending big suitcases straight Osaka → Tokyo hotel, and take only one-night bags to Kyoto. Confirm Tokyo hotel accepts forwarded luggage before sending.
🛍️ Tax-freeYour trip is still under the pre-Nov-1-2026 system: eligible shops can sell tax-free at checkout. Bring the original passport. Visit Japan Web can generate a tax-free QR for participating stores, but not every shop accepts it.
💴 CashCards work widely, but keep yen for smaller shops, temples, lockers and places such as Nakatanidou.
📷 Disposable cameraFun trip prop: buy a disposable film camera in Japan and use one roll across the whole trip. Check Donki / Bic Camera / Yodobashi rather than making a separate detour.

What you already bought is Universal Express Pass 4: Race & JAWS for 3 people. It is NOT the ticket that gets you through the park entrance. USJ officially requires a separate Studio Pass / 1-Day Studio Pass for admission. Think of it like this:

1-Day Studio Pass = enter USJ + normal access to attractions
Express Pass 4 = shorter queues + your timed/guaranteed attraction slots
